Your business doesn’t need an AI problem. It has business problems.

  • The quote that takes half a day.
  • The spreadsheet somebody rebuilds every Friday.
  • The same customer question answered again and again.
  • The information buried across emails and folders.
  • The process everyone knows is painful but nobody has fixed.

That’s where we start.

We help you work out what actually needs to change first, and whether AI needs to be part of the answer at all.

What should get better?

  • More sales
  • Better margins
  • Faster service
  • More capacity
  • Fewer mistakes
  • Better customer experience

That’s what we measure.

AI is simply one way we might get you there.

Your people know where the value is.

The people doing the work know where customers get frustrated, where sales opportunities disappear, where time gets wasted and which processes look simple on paper but aren’t. That’s why we involve them early.

We use AI to remove repetitive work, increase capacity and help people make better use of what they know. Not remove the judgement your business depends on.

A clever idea is still just an idea until it improves something that matters.

So how do we actually do this?

We start inside your business.

Look at the business

We talk to the people doing the work and look at where sales are being missed, customers are waiting, admin is piling up, information is getting stuck or people are repeating the same work.

Find what’s worth changing

We identify the opportunities that could make a meaningful difference and compare AI with simpler options.

Test it on real work

We use real work, real data and a small pilot to see what actually changes.

Put it to work and keep improving it

If it earns its place we help implement it, train the people using it and put sensible controls around it. Then we keep looking for the next thing worth doing.

Small first. Evidence next. Scale last.

Before we recommend scaling anything, we agree on what success should look like.

Hours saved, sales created, turnaround time reduced, rework reduced, customer response time improved, capacity gained.

If we can’t measure what changed, we haven’t proved very much.

Vlado Baban works across the journey from identifying an opportunity to turning it into something that can be built, used and scaled.

We study what happens when AI gets it wrong too.

Alongside our commercial work, we research real-world AI incidents. That means we see the predictable ways AI projects go wrong: misplaced trust, poor checking, weak controls, confusing ownership and technology used where it never really fitted the work.

We bring those lessons into the way we test AI, train people and put controls around it before small problems become expensive ones.
